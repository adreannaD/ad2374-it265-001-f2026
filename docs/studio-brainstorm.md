## Vision Statement
*What is the mission of your solo game studio? How does it align with creating and analyzing innovative game architectures?*

> Lumenbound Games creates innovative, mechanically-driven games that prioritize thoughtful systems, meaningful player choices, and experimentation. The studio focuses on designing and analyzing game architectures where individual mechanics work together to create engaging and memorable experiences.

---

## Core Values
*What principles guide your studio's approach to game design, architecture, and development?*

- Thoughtful Design: Every mechanic should serve a purpose and contribute to the overall player experience.
- Player-Centered Innovation: Experiment with new ideas while keeping games understandable and enjoyable for players.
- Strong Game Architecture: Build systems that are organized, maintainable, and capable of supporting complex interactions.
- Meaningful Mechanics: Create gameplay where player decisions have clear consequences and mechanics interact in interesting ways.
- Experimentation: Prototype unusual ideas and learn from both successful and unsuccessful designs.
- Accessibility and Clarity: Make complex systems understandable without removing their depth.
