Current Game Concept: Rapid Defense

Rapid Defense is a two-player physical card game where one player acts as an attacker creating threats, while the other player acts as a defender trying to protect a designated area.

The main design focus is creating meaningful decisions under pressure. The defender must identify incoming threats, decide which threat is most urgent, and choose the appropriate response.
